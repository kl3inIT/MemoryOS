# SEP490 weekly-report setup — design

## Outcome

Prepare five submission-ready weekly-report snapshots for SEP490_G53 and a separate, ten-week Linear planning view. The snapshots cover only the first five reporting weeks; weeks 6–10 remain planned work for later reports.

## Scope and source of truth

- The report workbooks live beside the supplied academic documents in `../../../../../docx/docs/docs-upload/`, not in the repository template directory. The original templates remain unchanged.
- The repository-facing explanation is [the SEP490 weekly-report plan](../../../academic/sep490/weekly-reports.md).
- The first two weeks are grounded in the supplied week-1 and week-2 meeting minutes. The later weeks are a proposed design-and-planning sequence derived from the SEP490 reference stages; they are not evidence of implementation or acceptance.
- The ten-week workbook is a proposed Linear setup, not the complete work record. Meeting minutes, academic documents, diagrams and test evidence record work that does not need a Linear issue. Named owners and deadlines are planning assignments until the group confirms them.

## Decisions

- Treat 07/09/2026–11/10/2026 as a five-week **reporting window**, not as a replacement for the project-wide timeline in `docs/academic/sep490/README.md`.
- Keep all current weekly status entries at `In Progress` or `Pending`. A report must not claim `Done`/`Completed` without specific evidence.
- Keep implementation, integrated testing and stabilization in weeks 6–10. The first five reports remain limited to discovery, requirements, design, test planning and iteration-1 refinement.

## Out of scope

- Creating or modifying issues in the hosted Linear workspace.
- Altering product implementation, capability contracts or the academic templates.
